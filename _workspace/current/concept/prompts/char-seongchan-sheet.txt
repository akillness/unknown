SUBJECT: a single full-body character concept sheet of a Korean man in his fifties, operator of a cold storage warehouse. He wears a tidy quilted vest over a shirt, clean boots, a slim analog thermometer clipped at his breast pocket. Hands open in an offering gesture, dry fabric, a small amount of verdigris tone in the vest. He is Korean, with East Asian features, black or salt-greyed dark hair; this is a fictional Korean port town. The pose is a clear three-quarter turn: the body rotated about thirty degrees away from the viewer, the face turned back toward the viewer, never a flat frontal stance.
