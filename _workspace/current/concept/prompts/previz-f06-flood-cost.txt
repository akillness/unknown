SUBJECT: previsualization frame, not gameplay. the lowland node: one plank walkway drained and dry with crates lifted onto blocks, and directly beside it the neighbouring walkway submerged, water lapping the house sills, salt stain lines on both walls.

STYLE: painterly semi-realistic game concept art for a quiet maritime-industrial mystery. Palette is strictly limited to deep ink #0E1F26, seal ink #173238, wet-metal blue-grey #36565C, verdigris bronze #4F7A6B, wet-concrete grey #8A8E88, salt-crystal pale cyan #C8D6D3, records-paper warm off-white #E7E3D8, and one single ochre accent #E2AF62 covering less than eight percent of the frame. Overcast night lighting from scattered low work lamps, low contrast, soft diffuse falloff. Every frame shows a dark core, a mid range and a bright range. MATERIALS, applied literally: salt crystal grows as fine hexagonal clusters at edges with matte scatter and pinpoint highlights; corroded bronze is a dark base with verdigris speckle and run marks that always flow downward; wet concrete reads twenty percent darker than dry concrete with a hard horizontal tide line marking past water level; brine conduit pipes have constant diameter, a white salt ring at every coupling, and round analog pressure dials. Nothing is new, nothing is glossy, everything has been worn by salt and water. Silhouette first: important shapes are separated from the background by a dark outline at least three pixels wide.

CAMERA: a fixed 2.5D observation node. 35mm equivalent lens, no barrel distortion, eye height 1.55 meters, pitch eighteen degrees downward, zero roll, no dutch angle, no implied camera movement. The horizontal water line sits in the upper 38 to 44 percent band of the frame. Large horizontal seawall masses, low ceilings, round gauges, architecture older than the people in it.

SEQUENCE CONSISTENCY: this is one frame of a nine frame previsualization sequence. Keep the identical palette, identical lamp colour and intensity, identical lens, identical node height and pitch, and the identical painterly rendering as every other frame in the sequence. Do not change art style between frames.

NEGATIVE: no text, no letters, no words, no numbers, no captions, no signage, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no signature, no laurel wreath, no award badge, no review score, no user-interface copied from any existing game, no real-world disaster photography, no weapons, no combat, no blood, no corpses, no ghosts, no apparitions, no supernatural glow, no anime style, no glossy CGI render, no neon, no lens flare, no bloom, no red warning lights, no chrome, no brand marks.
